A few things to know.

Where will my bags be stored?

The travel concept uses local businesses such as hotels, cafés and parcel shops. All locations in this demo are fictional.

Can I store bags for just a few hours?

Yes. Choose your drop-off and collection times. The quote uses hourly pricing with a cap for each 24-hour period.

How do photos and protection work?

Declare what you are storing, including contents you want protected. A closed-bag photo can identify the bag, not its contents. Photo analysis estimates visible belongings and their value for you to review. Protection is simulated in this prototype.

Can I book by voice?

A voice assistant is planned. The current Ask Luggage Mate control shows a scripted preview.
